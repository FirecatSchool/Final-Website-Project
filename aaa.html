Authentication Section:

The AAA Security Model
Authentication, Access Control & Accounting


The AAA model describes the three tools used in practice to achieve the CIA Triad.

Authentication

Prove who you are. Authentication is the establishment of trust between two parties.


Without trust, information cannot safely be shared or accessed.

Access Control

What are you allowed to see or reach? Also called authorization or permissions.


Not every authenticated user should access everything.

Accounting

Keep track of what happened. Also called auditing.


Logs and records let us review activity, detect anomalies, and respond to incidents.

Something you know (password or PIN)

Something you have (smart card, hardware token, code sent to your phone)
Something you are (fingerprint, retina scan, voice)
