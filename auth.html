Methods of authentication

modern security has gone far beyond Passwords
here are some of the key authentication methods.

Smart cards

Cards with an embedded chip

Digital certificits

servers use certificits to authenticate with browsers.

biometrics

Uses measurable physical characteristics.

hardware tokens

a physical device that generates a new one time password every 60 seconds.

2 factor authentication

combines two factors like a password and a pin sent to your phone.

multi factor authentication

use two or more different authentication factors

Single sign on

one authentication grants access to many servers or services.

Passphrases

A string of multiple words that are more secure than a long password.

Top 3 ways to stop password attacks:

  1. Strong passwords
  2. Enable acount lockout
  3. Enable MFA / 2FA
