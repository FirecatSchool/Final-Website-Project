Methods of authentication

modern security has gone far beyond Passwords
here are some of the key authentication methods.

Smart cards

Cards with an embedded chip reccognized by an authentication device/p>

Digital certificits

servers use certificits to authenticate with browsers.

biometrics

Uses measurable physical characteristics, fingerprint, retna scan, voice, or even typing speed.

hardware tokens

a physical device that generates a new one time password every 60 seconds. Intercepting it is useless to a hacker since the passwords are one time

2 factor authentication

combines two factors like a password and a pin sent to your phone. One stolen factor is useless.

multi factor authentication

use two or more different authentication factors, the gold standard for security.

Single sign on

one authentication grants access to many servers or services. Used widly in schools and businuses to reduce password fatigue.

Passphrases

A string of multiple words that are more secure than a long password. And is easier to remember.

Top 3 ways to stop password attacks:

  1. Strong passwords
  2. Enable acount lockout
  3. Enable MFA / 2FA
