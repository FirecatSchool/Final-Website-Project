Password attacks and how to defend against them:

Passwords are the most common, and most commonly attacked form of authentication.


Understanding the attack types is the first step to a solid defense.

Brute Force

Tests every possible combination until a match is found.

Dictionary Attack

Uses a large database of common words to rappidly guess passwords.

Password Spraying

Test one weak password against many accounts.

Credential Stuffing

Uses stolen usernames and passwords to attack other sites.
